Two ministers, one promise: your ceremony, your way.

The Wedding Padres began in 2021 in North Tonawanda, New York. We officiate weddings, baptisms and Holy Communion, and we welcome couples of every faith and background. Regardless of your religious beliefs or sexual orientation, we will build the ceremony you have always imagined.

Every service begins with a consultation. Frank learns your love story and what you want from the day, then combines your ideas with his experience. That can include family stories, scripture readings, sacred rituals and vows we write with you.

Frank and Ryan

Frank McGinley

Born in Niagara Falls. Frank is a Eucharistic Minister in the Catholic Church and is ordained through the Universal Life Church.

Ryan Brzyski

Ordained through the Universal Life Church and a member of the Catholic Church. Ryan is Frank’s partner in every ceremony.
